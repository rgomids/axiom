## Evaluation rule

Before adoption, evaluate at least:

- measurable benefit;
- architecture compatibility;
- local-first compatibility;
- maintenance activity;
- license;
- security/privacy impact;
- portability;
- coupling introduced;
- operational complexity;
- replacement cost.

Possible outcome:

```text
accepted
rejected
deferred
replaced
```

Do not convert a hypothesis into a dependency without an explicit decision.
