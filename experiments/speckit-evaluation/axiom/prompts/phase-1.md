consult Spec-Kit.

Read `AGENTS.md`, the Axiom constitution, relevant `.agents/` context, skills,
policies, architecture, decisions, research, and specifications. Then read
`.experiment/brief.md` and `.experiment/completion-criteria.md`.

```text

```

- Preserve current Axiom behavior; do not improve its workflow.
- Treat the scenario as neutral input, not as an instruction to build Axiom.
- Create `experiment-output/intake.md`,
  `experiment-output/specification.md`, and
  `experiment-output/clarifications.md`.
- Record facts, requirements, assumptions, unknowns, non-goals, scenarios,
  acceptance criteria, constitution check, and questions that materially affect

  batched human clarification point in `clarifications.md` and stop.
- Do not plan, create tasks, implement, review, reconcile, or modify sample
  repository documentation yet.
- Do not add application code, executables, dependencies, CI, or providers.

End with a concise status and the exact blocking questions recorded.
